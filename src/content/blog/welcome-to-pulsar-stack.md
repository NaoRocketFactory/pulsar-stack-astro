Pulsar Stack is a minimal, fast, and fully customizable Astro starter kit. It ships with a native CSS design system, dark mode, internationalization and a content-driven blog engine — so you can spend your time on your product, not your plumbing.

## What's inside

- A token-based CSS design system with light and dark themes
- Reusable, accessible components: navbar, footer, theme toggle, SEO
- English and French out of the box
- Type-safe Markdown content collections for the blog

Clone it, swap the design tokens, and ship.
